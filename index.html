Remedy Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Remedy Finder is an app for finding medicines in your own homeopathy medicine box by voice, photo and camera. It is made by Abdulrehman Shaukat ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • Your voice notes stay on your phone.
  • When you describe a health problem by voice, the recording is sent to an AI service to understand it and is not kept.
  • Medicine names and bottle photos you add are stored in our database so the app can show them.

What the app handles

InformationWhat happens to it
Voice notes you record for a medicineSaved and played on your phone only. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
Voice search ("say the medicine's name")Matched on your phone against your own voice notes. Your phone's speech recognition service (Apple or Google) may also turn the words into text.
Describing your problem by voice (symptoms)The recording is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini AI service to turn it into symptoms. It is processed only to answer that request and is not stored by us. This is health-related information.
Medicine details and bottle photos you addStored in our database (Supabase) so the app can list and show your medicines.
Anonymous app IDThe app signs in anonymously so it can read and save medicine details. There are no accounts, names, emails or passwords.
CameraUsed live on your phone to recognise the markers on your bottles. Camera frames are not uploaded. Photos are only uploaded when you take a bottle photo yourself.
The Remedy Finder tray (coming soon)The app talks to the tray directly on your home Wi-Fi to light up a slot. No personal information is sent to it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, messages, files, advertising ID or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or analytics code.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, file storage and server functions) and Google Gemini (to understand spoken symptom descriptions). They process data only on our behalf to provide these features. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Medical disclaimer

Remedy Finder is a reference and organising tool. It does not diagnose and is not medical advice. Please consult a qualified practitioner.

Children

Remedy Finder is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your Remedy Finder data

How to have your Remedy Finder data deleted:

  1. Voice notes: they are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling Remedy Finder deletes them immediately. Nothing to request.
  2. Medicine details and bottle photos you added: email abdulrehman.shaukat@gmail.com with the subject "Remedy Finder data deletion". We delete the medicine names, potencies and bottle photos you added within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  3. Spoken symptom descriptions: these are processed when you speak and are not stored, so there is nothing to delete.

We keep no other data about you. No backups of deleted data are retained.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
